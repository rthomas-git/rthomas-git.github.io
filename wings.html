Wings

Gameplay / AI / UI Programmer Student Project UE4 PC

Wings is a 3D side scroller action adventure game taking place in 5 environments, each with their own challenges. Traverse through the level destroying enemies and dodging nature's obstacles, all while collecting feathers and increasing your high score.

Platform: Windows PC Engine: UE4 Blueprints Duration: 6 Weeks Team Size: 5

Role Overview

  • Collaborated with designers and artists to implement collectibles, destruction mechanics, and progression systems.
  • Programmed UI elements for main characters and implemented health systems for enemies.
  • Worked with systems programmers to create the main save system, enhancing player experience and progression.
  • Contributed as a QA to test builds, player mechanics, combat mechanics, and AI.
