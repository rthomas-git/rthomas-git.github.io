Cactus Jim

Programmer / Designer / Artist Solo Project Unity PC

Cactus Jim is the first individual project I created to pass time one day, but it quickly sparked my love for game programming. In this platform minigame you play as Cactus Jim dodging different species of cacti — the longer you live, the higher your score.

Platform: Windows PC Engine: Unity C# Duration: ~1 Day
