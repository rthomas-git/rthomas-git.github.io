Camping Night

Gameplay / UI Programmer Student Project PROGRAMMER UE4 PC

Camping Night is a third person casual simulation game where you can experience the great adventures of the outdoors. Discover new people on the same journey and gain their friendship by completing tasks throughout the woods — but keep a close eye on your campfire!

Platform: Windows PC Engine: UE4 Blueprints Duration: 2.5 Weeks Team Size: 4

Role Overview

  • Orchestrated the comprehensive development of main player mechanics, ensuring a robust and responsive gameplay experience from conception to execution.
  • Formed a tight-knit collaboration with the designer to seamlessly integrate the Heads-Up Display (HUD), enhancing the player's interface and overall gaming immersion.
  • Collaborated with the design team to meticulously refine and implement various UI/UX elements, contributing to a polished and user-friendly visual design.
  • Played a vital role as a QA resource, actively testing builds and player mechanics, providing valuable insights to fine-tune and optimize the gaming experience.
