Fly South

Gameplay / AI / UI Programmer Student Project PROGRAMMER UNITY PC

Fly South is a top down shooter minigame where you play as a young lost blue jay (Jay) that has to migrate south for the winter — but along the way you will encounter flocks of crows and falcons that try to stop you.

Platform: Windows PC Engine: Unity C# Duration: 2 Weeks Team Size: 4

Role Overview

  • Developed core game mechanics from conceptualization to execution, ensuring a seamless and engaging player experience.
  • Engineered sophisticated enemy AI algorithms for both levels, enhancing the game's challenge and strategic depth.
  • Orchestrated the seamless integration of various level mechanics, ensuring a cohesive and immersive gameplay environment.
  • Crafted and implemented an intuitive main game HUD, providing players with essential information and enhancing overall UI design.
  • Designed and programmed an efficient game progression and save system, allowing players to navigate through the experience while ensuring progress is securely recorded.
