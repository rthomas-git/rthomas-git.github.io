Hazel: Tap Away Climate Change

Unity Developer October 2022 – January 2025 Unity Mobile

Come join Ralph and his friends as they combat climate change in their portion of the metaverse, the Hazelverse! Swipe away carbon, turn captured carbon into diamonds, earn upgrades and unlock Critters. You can also unlock your personal Hazel island and directly support carbon removal efforts in the real world through gameplay.
