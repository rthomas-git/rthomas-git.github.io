EA Sports College Football 25

Quality Analyst 6 Month Contract · January 2024 – July 2024 QA Testing PS5 XBOX SERIES X/S

After 11 years College Football has returned! It was a joy to be part of this team and to bring back one of the most anticipated sports titles from EA's catalogue. Still can't believe I was able to help successfully bring back a title that I played when I was a little kid.

Platform: PS5 & XSX/S Tools: JIRA, Zephyr, SQL Workbench, Confluence, Miro, Excel, Slack Released: July 15, 2024

Role Overview

  • Logged and triaged 200+ defects in JIRA and Zephyr over a 6-month contract, driving measurable improvements to software quality and contributing to on-time release milestones.
  • Validated content across multiple server environments and online modes (e.g., Ultimate Team) using EA internal tooling and SQL Workbench to confirm functionality and data integrity.
  • Partnered with product owners, engineers, designers, and QA leadership to define test coverage strategies using Confluence and Miro.
  • Executed manual and technical test passes on PS5 and XSX/S development kits, covering functional, regression, and exploratory testing.
  • Coordinated test execution across geographically distributed teams via Slack, tracking quality impact metrics in Power BI and Excel to support cross-functional alignment throughout the release cycle.
